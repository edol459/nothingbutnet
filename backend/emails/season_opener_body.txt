Opening night is here. Get on ydkball and start tracking the games you watch.

A few things are new since you last opened it:

**Pick your teams and make the profile yours**
When you come back you'll choose your favorite teams and players. Rep your team's badge and show how long you've been in the fan club.

**Player of the Game**
Call the best player in every game you log, then see whether the crowd agrees. The feed has a leaderboard of who's been picked most in the last month.

**Log while you watch**
No more waiting for the final buzzer. Start a note at tipoff, stamp the game clock when something happens, finish it at the end.

**Watchlist**
Follow a team and its games land on your schedule. Flag the ones you really don't want to miss.

**Your ticket stubs**
Games you went to in person now live on your profile as a collection you can flip through.

**Preseason ballots**
Call MVP, Rookie of the Year, the champion and more before the season starts. Locks at the first tipoff and grades itself when the awards are announced.

**The Vault**
Log any NBA game back to the 1983-84 season — for anyone who likes rewatching the classics.

See you there.
